Renaissance

14th-17th century · humanism, anatomy, and perspective

Rebirth of Classical Learning

The Renaissance marked a return to classical ideals, with artists obsessed with anatomy, proportion, and the illusion of depth.

Leonardo da Vinci used mathematical perspective and careful observation of the human body to construct believable, almost sculptural figures. His work reveals the tight relationship between art, science, and technology in this era.

  • Focus on realism, depth, and human emotion.
  • Use of linear perspective to organize space.
  • Religious narratives rendered with humanist sensitivity.
