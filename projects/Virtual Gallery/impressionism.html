Impressionism

19th century · light, atmosphere, and fleeting moments

Painting Light and Time

Impressionist painters broke away from academic rules to capture how scenes feel in a single moment: shimmering water, fog, and motion.

Claude Monet painted series like Water Lilies and Waterloo Bridge to show how light transforms color across time of day and season. In AR, these works become immersive environments.

  • Visible brushstrokes and softer edges.
  • Outdoor painting to observe natural light.
  • Focus on sensation instead of strict realism.
