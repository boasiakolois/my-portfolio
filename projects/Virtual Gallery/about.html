About the Project

The European AR Art Gallery is a Lois Boasiako's project for ISC 325. It combines web design, historical research, and emerging technologies to make European art more interactive and accessible.

Concept & Learning Goals

Visitors move across three major European art eras—Renaissance, Impressionism, and Modernism— while interacting with digital recreations and AR environments. Each page provides historical context, mini artist biographies, and links to museum archives and scholarly resources.

The project demonstrates how web technologies, audio narration, and AR tools can transform traditional art history content into an immersive learning experience.
