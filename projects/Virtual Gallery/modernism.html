Modernism

Late 19th-20th century · abstraction, fragmentation, and experimentation

Breaking the Picture Plane

Modernist artists questioned what an artwork should look like. Forms were flattened, fractured, and rearranged to show multiple viewpoints at once.

Pablo Picasso's Cubist works, such as Ma Jolie, reassemble reality into overlapping planes. In AR, these planes can float in space, allowing viewers to walk around ideas instead of just images.

  • Abstraction and geometric simplification.
  • Multiple perspectives in a single work.
  • Experiments with collage, mixed media, and symbolism.
